Verdict key: Fix when the part costs clearly less than the robot's used value and the job is routine. Sell for parts when the fault is a board, camera or sensor on a robot whose used value is low. Buying used

Inspect it in ten minutes

  • Run one full mop-wash cycle at the dock in front of you; it is the most expensive part to replace.
  • Ask the seller to remove the robot from the Roborock app.
  • Check the dock's clean-water tank for scale and the dirty-water tank for smell; both tell you about maintenance.
  • Confirm the dock ramp, mop pads, and dust bag are included.

Selling

Get the most for it

  • Run a dock self-clean, empty and dry both tanks, and replace the dust bag before photos.
  • Delete the robot from your app.
  • Photograph the dock interior; serious buyers ask for it.
  • Ship the dock and robot in separate boxes if you still have them; the dock is heavy enough to damage the robot in one box.
