      Methodology

      What counts as an observation. A completed sale or purchase of a specific model, with a date, a condition (working with dock; working without dock; needed a repair; for parts), a price, and a source we can name: one of our own flips, an owner's report, a manufacturer's refurbished-store price, or a public sold listing read by a person and logged with its URL. Asking prices are logged separately and never averaged into the band.

      How the band is computed. Median of working-with-dock observations in a rolling 90-day window, shown as a range from the 25th to the 75th percentile. Minimum three observations to publish; confidence is wide at 3-7, medium at 8-14, tight at 15 or more. A trend appears only after eight weekly snapshots exist.

      What we do not do. We do not scrape marketplaces, and we do not use data whose terms forbid commercial reuse. We have applied for eBay's Marketplace Insights programme; if it is granted, those observations will be labelled as such. We do not print retailer prices for new robots or parts because they change daily; buttons open the live listing.

      Corrections. If a figure is wrong, tell us. Corrections are logged on this page with the date.
