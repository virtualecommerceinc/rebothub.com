Privacy

What we collect

Reading the site: no cookies, no session recording. If we add analytics it will be cookieless, aggregate page counts only, and this page will say so.

Alerts: your email address, with double opt-in, held by our email provider. Every email has an unsubscribe link and our postal address. We do not sell or share the list.

Reports and listings: what you type in the form, held in a private spreadsheet, read by a person. We publish first name and month only. Do not send full serial numbers or home layouts; if you do, we remove them.

Outbound links: Amazon, eBay and manufacturer sites have their own policies. Affiliate links tell those sites that you came from RebotHub.

Your rights: ask us to delete anything you sent and we will. Contact via the report form.

Postal address and contact email will be added here before the alerts list goes live.
