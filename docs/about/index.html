      About

      Who checks this

      Dan builds things, films them, and runs the bots behind this site. He is not a licensed electrician or a manufacturer technician. Every guide shows its sources and its review status.

      Why RebotHub exists

      Most robot "failures" are a battery, a contact or a brush. Most robots that get thrown away could have been fixed for a fraction of their price or sold to someone who would fix them. And the first used humanoids are a few years away with nobody writing down what will make one worth buying. RebotHub is the second-life guide for the robots people own now, and the notebook for the ones that are coming.

      How we work

      • Guides carry a provenance badge. "Hands-on" means Dan Herlehy did the repair on camera. "Documented" means the steps follow a manufacturer's published procedure. Nothing is published on AI inference alone.
      • Values carry a sample size and a date. Fewer than three observations and the page says "collecting".
      • Drafts are prepared with AI assistance and reviewed by a person; every page says whether that review has happened yet.
      • We earn commissions on some links and say so directly above them. Verdicts are written before any link is chosen.
