Roborock "Error 1" (lidar blocked): clean the turret before you buy anything

The robot says "Error 1" and will not start cleaning; the turret may not spin.

Documented: manufacturer or published sourceRisk: lowTools: Microfibre cloth, Phillips screwdriver (for a module swap)

The Roborock Q7 Max manual lists Error 1 as "LiDAR turret or laser blocked. Check for obstruction and retry." The open-source python-roborock library maps code 1 to the same condition. Some third-party sites describe Error 1 differently; we follow the manual.

Drafted with AI assistance from the sources named above. Not yet reviewed by Dan Herlehy; it will carry a review date when it has been.

🔧 Verdict: FixFix. Most Error 1 reports are a hair, a sticker, or a chair rung stopping the turret. If the turret will not spin freely with nothing in the way, the lidar module is a moderate-cost part and a medium-difficulty swap; get a used price for the whole robot before committing.

Steps

  1. Lift the robot away from furniture. Look at the raised lidar turret on top: there is a bumper ring around it. Remove anything touching the ring.
  2. Gently spin the turret by hand. It should turn freely. Wipe the dark window around it with a dry microfibre cloth.
  3. Check that the turret's pressure bumper is not stuck down; press it and let it spring back.
  4. Press the power button to restart and run a short spot clean.
  5. If the turret does not spin on startup with nothing touching it, the lidar motor or belt has failed. Replacement lidar modules exist for the S7 family and the swap is a top-cover-off job; compare the part cost against the robot's used value before deciding.
