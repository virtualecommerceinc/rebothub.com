      Fix guide

      How to sell a used robot for the most money, and ship it without drama

      You want to sell a robot and get a fair price without a return.

      Editorial guidance, not a repairRisk: battery

      General guidance drawn from marketplace practice. Battery shipping rules are the carrier's, and they change; check yours before you print a label.

      Drafted with AI assistance from the sources named above. Not yet reviewed by Dan Herlehy; it will carry a review date when it has been.

      ✓ Bottom lineA clean robot with a fresh filter, a stated runtime and proof of account removal sells faster and for more than the same robot listed dirty.
      Safety first · battery workNever ship a swollen or damaged lithium battery. Remove it and take it to a recycling point; sell the robot as battery-not-included. Unplug the robot and remove it from its dock before opening anything. Never puncture, bend, heat, or charge a swollen or damaged lithium battery. If a pack is swollen, stop and take it to a battery recycling point. Use the manufacturer's part number or a pack the manufacturer lists as compatible. You take on the risk of work you do yourself; if anything looks damaged or you are unsure, stop.

      Steps

      1. Clean it properly: bin, filter, brushes, sensor windows, dock contacts. Replace the filter and side brush if worn; they cost less than the price difference.
      2. Charge it fully and run it. Write down the runtime you actually saw and the date. Put that sentence in the listing.
      3. Photograph it from above, below, the dock, the contacts and every included accessory. Hide serial numbers.
      4. Describe faults plainly. A known fault stated upfront is a discount; a known fault discovered later is a return.
      5. Remove the robot from your account, then reset. Say so in the listing.
      6. Price it against recent sold listings, not asking prices. RebotHub's value pages show the observations we have collected and their dates.
      7. Shipping: keep the battery installed and the robot switched off; a lithium battery inside equipment generally ships under simpler rules than a loose pack, but your carrier decides. Large packs, like a legged robot's, need the proper lithium label.
      8. Drain water tanks completely. Tape the bumper so it cannot be pressed in transit. Ship docks in a separate box if you have one.

      Questions

      Should I list on RebotHub's market or on eBay?

      Today, eBay, because that is where the buyers are. Our market page gives you a pre-filled title and condition template. When our listings page has real volume we will say so.
