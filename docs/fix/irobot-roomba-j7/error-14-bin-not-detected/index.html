Roomba "Error 14" (bin not detected): a two-minute fix most of the time

Roomba says "Error fourteen" or "Error one four" and will not start.

Documented: manufacturer or published sourceRisk: lowTools: Cotton swab, Isopropyl alcohol

iRobot's support article states that Error 14 or 1-4 means the robot is not detecting an installed bin, and that a replacement bin may be needed if the message persists. The 900 series says "fourteen"; the 600 and 800 series say "one four".

Drafted with AI assistance from the sources named above. Not yet reviewed by Dan Herlehy; it will carry a review date when it has been.

🔧 Verdict: FixFix. Nine times out of ten this is a dirty contact. The worst case is a replacement bin, which is still a low-cost part.

Steps

  1. Remove the bin, wait five seconds, and push it back in until it clicks. Press CLEAN.
  2. If the error returns, remove the bin and look at the two small metal contacts on the bin and the matching pair on the robot. Wipe both with a dry cloth or a cotton swab with a little isopropyl alcohol. Let it dry.
  3. Check the bin for a cracked tab; a bin that sits a millimetre too low will not register.
  4. If the robot still reports Error 14 with a clean, fully seated bin, try a known-good bin from the same series. If that clears it, replace the bin. If it does not, the sensor on the robot side is at fault; that is a sell-for-parts decision on an older robot.

Questions

Does Error 14 mean the filter is full?

No. A full bin gives a different message. Error 14 is specifically about the robot not sensing that a bin is installed.

Applies to: Roomba j7 and j7+, Roomba i7 and i7+, Roomba 960.
