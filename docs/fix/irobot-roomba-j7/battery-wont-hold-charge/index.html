      Roomba battery won't hold a charge: replace it in 20 minutes

      Short runtime, early return to dock, or a robot that dies mid-room.

      Documented: manufacturer or published sourceRisk: batteryTools: Phillips screwdriver

      Based on iRobot's published battery-replacement procedure and widespread owner reports. Not yet performed on camera by RebotHub; it is on the flip list.

      Drafted with AI assistance from the sources named above. Not yet reviewed by Dan Herlehy; it will carry a review date when it has been.

      🔧 Verdict: FixFix. A battery is the cheapest repair that restores the most value. If a new battery does not help, the fault is elsewhere and this robot moves to the sell-for-parts column.
      Safety first · battery workLithium battery. Never puncture, crush, or heat the old pack. If it is swollen, do not press it back in; take it to a battery recycling point. Do not use third-party packs that lack a capacity and voltage marking. Unplug the robot and remove it from its dock before opening anything. Never puncture, bend, heat, or charge a swollen or damaged lithium battery. If a pack is swollen, stop and take it to a battery recycling point. Use the manufacturer's part number or a pack the manufacturer lists as compatible. You take on the risk of work you do yourself; if anything looks damaged or you are unsure, stop.

      Steps

      1. Confirm the diagnosis first: fully charge on the dock overnight, then run it. If it quits in under 20 minutes on a clean floor, the battery is the likely cause. If it dies instantly or never shows a charging light, read the not-charging guide first.
      2. Power off and flip the robot. Remove the bin and the edge-sweeping brush.
      3. Undo the screws holding the bottom cover. On the j and i series they are captive; loosen, do not pull.
      4. Lift the old pack straight out. Note the connector orientation before you disconnect anything.
      5. Fit the new pack, reconnect, close the cover, and tighten the captive screws until snug.
      6. Place the robot on the dock and leave it for a full charge before the first run. Several short cycles will follow as the robot learns the new pack.

      Questions

      How long should a Roomba battery last?

      Owners commonly report two to three years of daily use before runtime drops noticeably. Storage habits matter: a Roomba left off its dock for months ages faster.

      Can I just buy a new robot instead?

      You can, but a battery costs a small fraction of a replacement robot and restores most of the lost runtime. A new robot is the better answer only when several other parts are also worn.
