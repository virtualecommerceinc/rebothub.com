      Roomba "Error 1" and "Error 6": stuck wheels and dirty cliff sensors

      "Error one, move Roomba to a new location" or "Error six" on a dark rug.

      Documented: manufacturer or published sourceRisk: lowTools: Dry cloth, Compressed air

      iRobot's 800 and 900 series error chart: Error 1 means the robot is stuck or a wheel is hanging down; Error 6 means the cliff sensors are dirty or the robot is hanging over a drop or on a dark surface. Codes can differ on newer models.

      Drafted with AI assistance from the sources named above. Not yet reviewed by Dan Herlehy; it will carry a review date when it has been.

      🔧 Verdict: FixFix, usually with no parts at all. If Error 1 persists on a flat floor with a free wheel, the wheel module is the next suspect and is a moderate-cost part.

      Steps

      1. Error 1: pick the robot up, set it on flat open floor, and press CLEAN. If it now runs, it was simply stuck.
      2. Error 1 again on a flat floor: flip the robot and press each drive wheel in. It should move freely and spring back. A wheel that sticks down or feels gritty needs its module cleaned or replaced.
      3. Error 6: flip the robot and wipe the four cliff sensor windows along the front underside with a dry cloth. Compressed air helps.
      4. Error 6 on a dark rug: that is the sensors working as designed. iRobot has sold a dark-floor firmware behaviour for some models; otherwise keep the robot off very dark rugs or use the app to mark them as a keep-out zone.
      5. If Error 6 persists on a light, clean floor with clean sensor windows, a cliff sensor has failed. On a 960 that is a sell-for-parts decision.

      Questions

      Is Error 6 a fault if it only happens on one rug?

      No. The sensors are reading the dark surface as a drop. Mark the rug as a keep-out zone or lift it.

      Applies to: Roomba 960, Roomba i7 and i7+.
