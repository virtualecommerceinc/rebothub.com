      Roomba not charging on its dock: contacts, dock, then battery

      No charging light, or the robot sits on the dock and stays dead.

      Documented: manufacturer or published sourceRisk: batteryTools: Dry cloth

      Follows iRobot's published troubleshooting order (contacts, dock placement and power, then battery). Not yet performed on camera by RebotHub.

      Drafted with AI assistance from the sources named above. Not yet reviewed by Dan Herlehy; it will carry a review date when it has been.

      🔧 Verdict: FixFix, in this order: clean contacts, prove the dock has power, then suspect the battery. Only if all three check out is the main board in question, and then the robot moves to sell-for-parts.
      Safety first · battery workIf the battery is swollen, do not try to charge it. Remove it and take it to a recycling point. Unplug the robot and remove it from its dock before opening anything. Never puncture, bend, heat, or charge a swollen or damaged lithium battery. If a pack is swollen, stop and take it to a battery recycling point. Use the manufacturer's part number or a pack the manufacturer lists as compatible. You take on the risk of work you do yourself; if anything looks damaged or you are unsure, stop.

      Steps

      1. Check the dock. Its indicator light should blink when first plugged in. Try a different outlet. A dock that shows nothing with a known-good outlet is the problem, and docks are cheap used.
      2. Wipe the two charging contacts under the robot and the two on the dock with a dry cloth, then a lightly dampened one. Dust and pet hair build an insulating film.
      3. Place the dock against a wall on a hard floor with clear space in front, and set the robot on it by hand. Watch for the charging indicator within a minute.
      4. If the light appears but the robot still will not run, charge overnight and then follow the battery guide.
      5. If a new battery and a proven dock still produce nothing, the charging circuit on the main board is the likely fault. On an i7 or older that is a sell-for-parts decision; on a j7 get a quote before deciding.

      Questions

      Why does it charge for a while and then stop?

      That pattern usually points to a worn battery or dirty contacts rather than the dock. Clean the contacts first; it costs nothing.

      Applies to: Roomba i7 and i7+, Roomba j7 and j7+, Roomba 960.
