## Listings, trust, and operational interface

Market's confirmed direction is free initial listings and an intermediary connecting buyers and sellers, with optional paid higher placement later. Hub should make that relationship clear wherever it sends visitors into commerce. Payments, escrow, warranties, financing, and transaction handling are not approved. A low-liability aspiration is not a no-liability guarantee.

Proposed shared operating checklist:

- Identify who reviews submissions, verifies necessary contact details, handles reports, and corrects misleading material.
- Track seller permission and inventory provenance; distinguish submitted listings, partner inventory, and clearly labeled external links.
- Define duplicate detection, sold/withdrawn states, stale-listing expiry, removal requests, and a realistic review response process.
- Collect only needed personal information; establish which fields are public, private, retained, or deleted. Do not automatically publish full serial numbers.
- Treat condition descriptions, battery hazards, account locks, ownership rights, service transferability, and truthful availability as planning considerations requiring appropriate expertise.
- Obtain suitable review of terms, privacy, intellectual-property handling, and responsibility boundaries before launch; no legal conclusion is supplied here.

Historical exploration of Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, or eBay sellers does not authorize current outreach, scraping, republication, or data collection. No seller has been contacted for this task.
